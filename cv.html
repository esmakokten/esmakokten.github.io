Research Projects

Hypervisor Support for Efficient Isolation between Critical Computations and VMs2025 – present

Sensitive computations, such as operations involving TLS private keys, need protection from potentially compromised software that uses them. Separating these computations from a VM introduces communication overhead that can make frequent interactions expensive. This project develops hypervisor support in Composite for running critical computations as isolated, preemptible, schedulable services outside the VM, with low-overhead invocation.

  • Implemented the mechanism in C and x86-64 assembly across the Composite microkernel, its Intel VT-x hypervisor, and the Linux guest kernel.
  • Evaluated TLS key isolation with nginx, keeping private keys in an external service that the VM can invoke without directly accessing the keys.
  • Measured a 942-cycle guest-to-service round trip on an Intel Xeon Platinum 8160, approximately 1.4× the measured cost of a bare VM exit and entry, and below KVM’s in-kernel handling of the same call (1,709 cycles tuned, 4,202 stock).
Shielded Processor Reservations: Predictable CPU Allocation under Adversarial WorkloadsRTAS 2025

CPU reservations promise predictable access to processing time, but malicious tasks can undermine these guarantees by triggering excessive reservation bookkeeping, timer interrupts, and repeated preemption. SPR addresses both timely access to reserved CPU time and the ability to use that time for meaningful computation. Its design processes at most two reservations per scheduler invocation and supports non-preemptive execution regions to mitigate preemption storms. With Gabriel Parmer and Björn Brandenburg (MPI-SWS).

  • Implemented SPR and baseline reservation policies in Composite and conducted the full experimental evaluation of scheduling overhead and temporal isolation under adversarial workloads.
  • Demonstrated that 2 ms non-preemptive regions restored matrix-multiplication throughput under a timer-flood attack to 99.6% of the no-interference baseline.
